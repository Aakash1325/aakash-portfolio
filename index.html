MOBILITY · APP CONCEPT

Parking Spot

A parking app concept that helps people plan their arrival before the last-minute search begins.

A better arrival starts before the drive.

The problem

At unfamiliar destinations, drivers often start looking for parking only when they arrive. They have little time to understand which spaces are available or compare their options.

My solution

I proposed an app that brings public, operated and homeowner spaces into one place, with useful details for comparing them before the journey. I designed the website first to communicate the product direction ahead of the app build.

Why it matters

Drivers could choose a suitable space before setting out and arrive with a clearer plan. Homeowners could make unused spaces easier to discover.

HR TECH · WEBSITE REDESIGN

Vidmagin

A responsive redesign that makes Vidmagin’s product value and next steps clearer to prospective teams.

Making a complex product easier to understand.

The problem

The website needed to explain a broad hiring product without making visitors work through an overloaded page.

My solution

Why it matters

Prospective teams get a clearer path through the product information, while developers have a more consistent design direction to build from.
