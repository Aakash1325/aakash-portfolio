MOBILITY · APP CONCEPT

Parking Spot

Independent concept · product direction and website design

An independent parking app concept, introduced through a responsive website while the app is still planned.

A better arrival starts before the drive.

The problem

At unfamiliar destinations, drivers often start looking for parking only when they arrive. They have little time to understand which spaces are available or compare their options.

My response

I proposed one place to discover public, operated and homeowner spaces, with useful information for comparing options before a journey. I designed the website first to explain this direction ahead of the app build.

Intended value

The intended value is a clearer arrival plan for drivers and a way for homeowners to make unused spaces discoverable. This is a product hypothesis, not a measured outcome.

HR TECH · WEBSITE REDESIGN

Vidmagin

UI/UX Designer · website redesign

A responsive website redesign focused on making Vidmagin’s product value and next steps clearer.

Making a complex product easier to understand.

The problem

The website needed to explain a broad hiring product without asking visitors to work through an overloaded page.

My response

I reorganised the content into a clearer sequence, designed responsive layouts and established reusable visual patterns for implementation.

Intended value

The redesign gives prospective teams a more direct path through product information and gives developers a more consistent design direction. No conversion or usability lift is claimed here.

B2B EXPORT · WEB CONCEPT

AakashAgro

Independent concept · website design

A B2B export website concept centred on product information, sourcing context and enquiries.

Building trust before the first enquiry.

The problem

Trade buyers need enough context about products, sourcing and supplier credibility before starting a conversation.

My response

I organised the concept around product categories, sourcing context and a direct enquiry path, with a visual direction that supports the information.

Intended value

The intended experience helps buyers find relevant details and make a more informed enquiry. This concept has no measured business outcome.
