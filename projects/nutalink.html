01 / BACKGROUND & CHALLENGE

Setting the direction.

What the project addresses

NutaLink is an agricultural B2B platform. The work calls for a consistent interface across product discovery and business-oriented journeys.

The design challenge

A business network needs a clear introduction and consistent responsive behavior. I treated the relationship between interface design and browser implementation as a central design challenge; no user-study result is implied.

WHAT THE DESIGN AIMS TO DO

Three useful objectives.

01

Introduce the proposition

03

Use reusable interface structures across the designed experience.
