01 / BACKGROUND & CHALLENGE

Setting the direction.

What the project addresses

FITTEX is an established ERP for apparel and textile businesses. The public site introduces the platform; the software supports operational work behind it.

The design challenge

An established apparel ERP spans many operational areas. The design challenge was to introduce its breadth on a public site while making selected working interfaces easier to scan and use. This is a product-design framing, not a claim from a formal study.

WHAT THE DESIGN AIMS TO DO

Three useful objectives.

01

Explain the platform

Lead a visitor from the core proposition to capabilities and a practical next step.

02

Organize dense work

Give selected dashboards, reports, forms and billing screens a readable hierarchy.

03

Support continuity

Carry useful patterns across responsive marketing and product surfaces.

02 / AUDIENCE & STRATEGY

Designed around intent.

The public site follows an overview-to-detail sequence. Within the existing ERP, I worked on selected interfaces where grouping, labels and reusable components support repeated business tasks.

Prospective apparel businesses

Understand the scope of the platform and decide whether to enquire.

ERP users

Find information and next actions within the operational areas I contributed to.

KEY DESIGN DECISIONS

The reasoning behind the work.

These are design intentions. No measured outcome is implied.

01
DESIGN CHALLENGE

Broad product scope

DECISION

Sequence the public story from promise to capabilities

WHY

A visitor needs context before feature depth

INTENDED BENEFIT

An easier route toward a relevant enquiry

02
DESIGN CHALLENGE

Dense operational information

DECISION

Use hierarchy in selected ERP dashboards, reports and forms

WHY

Repeated work needs predictable emphasis

INTENDED BENEFIT

Faster scanning as an intended benefit

03
DESIGN CHALLENGE

Different screen sizes

DECISION

Adapt the website and contributed interfaces responsively

WHY

Business tasks and product evaluation happen across devices

INTENDED BENEFIT

Readable content and actions at each width

What I delivered.

Current state

What comes next
