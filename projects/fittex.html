Public-facing FITTEX website screenshot.

THE CONTEXT

What this
work is about.

FITTEX is an established ERP for apparel and textile businesses. The public site introduces the platform; the software supports operational work behind it.

MY CONTRIBUTION

What I worked
on.

I designed the current public website, including its structure, product presentation, responsive layouts, navigation, and calls to action. Within the existing ERP, I designed new screens and redesigned selected screens spanning dashboards, reports, forms, listings, subscriptions, billing, and account interfaces.

THE THINKING BEHIND THE WORK

Three design
considerations.

01 / DESIGN CONSIDERATION

Present the platform in a readable sequence

The public website moves from the product promise into capabilities and next steps, giving visitors a route through a broad software offering.

02 / DESIGN CONSIDERATION

Design for business tasks

My ERP contribution covered selected operational interfaces, where information hierarchy, forms, and repeatable patterns matter.

03 / DESIGN CONSIDERATION

Carry consistency across surfaces

I worked with responsive layouts and interface patterns so the public site and selected product screens feel coherent.

CURRENT STATE & LIMITS

What this work
can show.

The website is live. The screenshot documents the public experience; private ERP screens are intentionally excluded. No conversion or product performance result is claimed.

A fuller case study would pair approved ERP examples with implementation notes and evidence from users or product analytics.
