Parking Spot

Plan the arrival before the drive.

A parking app concept introduced through a responsive website; the app remains planned.

PROJECT TYPEIndependent product concept
STATUSWebsite designed; app planned
CONTRIBUTIONIndependent product direction and website design.
01 / Challenge02 / Approach03 / Design04 / Delivery
01 / BACKGROUND & CHALLENGE

Setting the direction.

What the project addresses

At unfamiliar destinations, drivers may need a better way to compare parking options before arriving.

The design challenge

Drivers at unfamiliar destinations may want to compare parking options before arrival. Parking Spot explores that opportunity as a concept; it has not been validated with users or launched as a booking service.

WHAT THE DESIGN AIMS TO DO

Three useful objectives.

01

Support planning

Introduce the idea of discovering options ahead of a trip.

02

Explain the choices

Differentiate public, operated and homeowner spaces conceptually.

03

Communicate honestly

Use a website to explain the future service without implying that the app already works.

02 / AUDIENCE & STRATEGY

Designed around intent.

The website tells the story of the proposed service and the intended decision sequence. It is a communication piece for a planned application, not a functioning inventory or booking tool.

Drivers (proposed audience)

Consider destination parking before setting out.

Space providers (proposed audience)

Understand the types of spaces the concept could include.

04 / IMPLEMENTATION & REFLECTION

What I delivered.

My contribution

I set the independent product direction and designed the responsive storytelling website. The application and its booking behavior remain planned.

Current state

This is a product hypothesis and website design. The app and user outcomes have not been validated.

What comes next

Test whether drivers understand the proposed categories and what information they need to trust a space before building the app.
