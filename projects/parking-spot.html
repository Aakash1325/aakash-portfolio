Plan the arrival before the drive.

A parking app concept introduced through a responsive website; the app remains planned.

ROLE

Independent concept and website designer

OWNERSHIP

Independent product direction and website design.

STATUS

Website designed; app planned

Visual: Public-facing Parking Spot concept artwork.

01 / CONTEXT

The setting

At unfamiliar destinations, drivers may need a better way to compare parking options before arriving.

02 / CONTRIBUTION

What I worked on

I proposed a discovery experience spanning public, operated, and homeowner spaces, and designed the website to communicate the concept ahead of an app build.

03 / APPROACH

Design considerations

01 / DESIGN CONSIDERATION

Start before arrival

The concept favors planning while there is time to compare options.

02 / DESIGN CONSIDERATION

Bring options together

Different space types can be considered in one discovery journey.

03 / DESIGN CONSIDERATION

Explain before building

The website communicates a proposed service, not a working booking flow.

04 / CURRENT STATE

What this shows

This is a product hypothesis and website design. The app and user outcomes have not been validated.

05 / NEXT STEP

What I’d explore

The next step is to test search, comparison, availability, and trust in the proposed journey.
