A better arrival starts before the drive.

An independent parking app concept, introduced through a responsive website while the app is still planned.

MY ROLE

Independent concept · product direction and website design

STATUS

Website designed · app concept

THE CONTEXT

What needed
to change.

At unfamiliar destinations, drivers often start looking for parking only when they arrive. They have little time to understand which spaces are available or compare their options.

MY RESPONSE

How I approached
the design.

I proposed one place to discover public, operated and homeowner spaces, with useful information for comparing options before a journey. I designed the website first to explain this direction ahead of the app build.

THE THINKING BEHIND THE SCREEN

Three decisions
that shaped it.

01 / DESIGN DECISION

Start before arrival

The concept focuses on choosing a suitable space while planning the journey, when a driver has time to compare options.

02 / DESIGN DECISION

Bring different spaces together

Public, operated and homeowner spaces are considered in one discovery experience, so availability and suitability can be compared in context.

03 / DESIGN DECISION

Explain the direction first

The responsive website communicates the proposed service before the app exists; it is not presented as proof of a working booking flow.

INTENDED VALUE & CURRENT LIMITS

What this work
can show.

The intended value is a clearer arrival plan for drivers and a way for homeowners to make unused spaces discoverable. This is a product hypothesis, not a measured outcome.

The next design step is to map and test the search, comparison and booking journey, including how space availability and trust would work in practice.
